Rooted in Ntujia, Marimanti Ward, Tharaka South, Tharaka Nithi County, CAVIMA promotes practical, inclusive and sustainable community action.

CAVIMA is a community-based, non-political and non-profit organization established for lawful community development purposes.

Our community commitment

We combine local knowledge with practical training, responsible partnerships and evidence-informed action. Our aim is to strengthen community capacity so that people can participate in solutions, protect natural resources and pursue resilient livelihoods.

Responding to climate pressure

Climate variability can affect rainfall, crop calendars, water availability, pasture and soil health. CAVIMA promotes adaptation that is practical, locally appropriate and based on credible technical guidance.

Productivity and resilience

Climate-smart practices can seek to maintain or improve productivity while strengthening resilience and reducing avoidable environmental pressure. Options include soil cover, water conservation, diversified production and improved farm planning.

Soil health

Healthy soils support water retention, nutrient cycling and crop productivity. Farmers can learn about organic matter, erosion control, mulching, appropriate fertility management and reduced unnecessary soil disturbance.

Water efficiency

Water-saving practices may include harvesting rainwater where feasible, protecting catchments, improving irrigation efficiency, scheduling water use carefully and selecting crops suited to available water.

Trees and biodiversity

Agroforestry and tree planting can support soil protection, biodiversity, shade and other ecosystem benefits. Species selection should consider local suitability and competition with crops and water resources.

Climate information and planning

Farmers benefit from timely information about weather, seasonal forecasts and agronomic recommendations. CAVIMA can help translate technical messages into practical planning conversations.

Community resilience

Climate-smart agriculture is linked to livelihoods, savings, diversification, collective action and preparedness. Stronger households and groups are better positioned to manage shocks.

Working with CAVIMA

We welcome constructive engagement with farmers, community groups, schools, youth and women groups, researchers, volunteers, government extension structures, civil society organizations, private-sector actors and development partners. Any formal collaboration is subject to appropriate agreement, roles, approvals and documentation.
