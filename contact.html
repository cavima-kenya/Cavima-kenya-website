Whether you are a community organization, farmer group, researcher, institution, development partner or community member, contact CAVIMA to discuss training, collaboration, research, outreach or community-development opportunities.

How to contact CAVIMA

Use the official contact details below for enquiries. A clear message helps the organization understand your request and respond appropriately.

Training requests

Tell us the topic, target group, expected participants, location, preferred period and practical challenge the training should address.

Partnership requests

Share your organization profile, proposed collaboration, target community, expected contribution and intended outcomes.

Community enquiries

Community members can ask about activities, participation opportunities, agricultural learning and community initiatives.

Our commitment

CAVIMA aims to communicate respectfully and transparently. Requests are considered according to programme priorities, capacity, technical requirements and available resources.
