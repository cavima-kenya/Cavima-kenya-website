Protect Nature. Empower People. Transform Community.

Rooted in Ntujia, Marimanti Ward, Tharaka South, Tharaka Nithi County, CAVIMA promotes practical, inclusive and sustainable community action.

CAVIMA is a community-based, non-political and non-profit organization established for lawful community development purposes.

Our community commitment

We combine local knowledge with practical training, responsible partnerships and evidence-informed action. Our aim is to strengthen community capacity so that people can participate in solutions, protect natural resources and pursue resilient livelihoods.

Community development

We support community-led identification of needs and opportunities, then help groups plan realistic activities. Our work can include livelihoods, environmental action, social support, savings culture, training, mentorship and community mobilization.

Agriculture and food security

We promote practical farming knowledge, sustainable production, crop diversification, tree nurseries, soil and water conservation, household food production and learning around nutrition and local value chains.

Capacity building

CAVIMA can train community organizations, farmer groups, youth groups and other local structures in organizational skills, leadership, record keeping, project planning, basic financial management, agriculture and community participation.

Research and innovation

We encourage small, practical studies and learning exercises that help communities understand what works. Evidence from community experience can inform improved farming practices, training methods, project design and resource mobilization.

Youth and women empowerment

We support inclusive participation by creating opportunities for skills, mentorship, entrepreneurship, leadership, agriculture, environmental action and community service.

Environmental conservation

Activities may include tree planting, nursery establishment, biodiversity awareness, recycling initiatives, soil protection, water conservation and community education on sustainable resource use.

Outreach and partnerships

We take information and engagement closer to communities through meetings, demonstrations and group discussions, while building partnerships that can expand technical support and learning opportunities.

Working with CAVIMA

We welcome constructive engagement with farmers, community groups, schools, youth and women groups, researchers, volunteers, government extension structures, civil society organizations, private-sector actors and development partners. Any formal collaboration is subject to appropriate agreement, roles, approvals and documentation.
