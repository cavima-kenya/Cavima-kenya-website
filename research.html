CAVIMA Research

People, knowledge and practical action.

CAVIMA promotes research that starts with real community questions and seeks useful, understandable and responsible solutions.

Community-based research

Research can help communities understand agricultural, environmental, livelihood and organizational challenges. Community participation can improve relevance and help connect findings with lived experience.

Agricultural research

Potential areas include crop productivity, soil health, water management, climate resilience, agricultural technologies, post-harvest losses, markets, value addition and adoption of improved practices.

Research-to-action

CAVIMA seeks to translate useful findings into training, demonstrations, information materials and community discussions so that evidence can influence practical decisions.

Innovation

Innovation may involve technology, improved farming methods, new enterprise models, digital information or better ways of organizing community action. Solutions should be appropriate to the context and resources available.

Monitoring and learning

Monitoring can help identify what is working, what needs improvement and what lessons should be shared. Learning should influence future planning.

Research integrity

CAVIMA aims to distinguish documented results from aspirations. Research findings, statistics, project achievements and partnerships should be presented accurately and supported by appropriate evidence.

Work with CAVIMA

We welcome responsible conversations with community organizations, farmers, researchers, institutions and development partners interested in practical and sustainable community development.
