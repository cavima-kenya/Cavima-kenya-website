CAVIMA Climate

People, knowledge and practical action.

CAVIMA promotes environmental responsibility and climate-smart agricultural approaches that help communities manage natural resources while strengthening livelihoods.

Climate-smart agriculture

Communities can explore practices that improve resilience and productivity while using land and water responsibly. Examples include diversification, agroforestry, soil conservation and efficient irrigation.

Tree growing and nurseries

Community nurseries can support tree growing and agroforestry. Good nursery management includes suitable propagation, watering, pest observation, hardening and responsible planting.

Soil conservation

Protecting soil is essential for agriculture. Vegetative cover, mulching, organic matter and appropriate erosion-control approaches can help maintain productive land.

Water conservation

Water harvesting, storage, efficient irrigation and responsible use can help communities make better use of available water.

Environmental education

Awareness can cover waste management, biodiversity, tree care, land stewardship and the connection between environmental health and livelihoods.

Community resilience

Resilience can be strengthened through diversified livelihoods, improved agricultural practices, preparedness, community learning and long-term natural-resource stewardship.

Work with CAVIMA

We welcome responsible conversations with community organizations, farmers, researchers, institutions and development partners interested in practical and sustainable community development.
