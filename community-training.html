CAVIMA • Community Training

A long-form programme perspective.

Turning information into practical skills for farmers, groups, youth, women and community organizations. This page is intentionally detailed so visitors can understand the organization, its methods and the opportunities for participation.

01. Needs assessment

Needs assessment is an important part of CAVIMA’s wider community-development model. The organization can approach this area through participation, practical learning, responsible planning, documentation and collaboration. Activities should be adapted to community priorities, available resources, technical requirements and applicable Kenyan laws and procedures.

02. Farmer field learning

Training should be practical and participatory. CAVIMA can combine short presentations with demonstrations, exercises, field observation, peer exchange and follow-up. The goal is not only attendance but improved knowledge, confidence and practical application.
