Rooted in Ntujia, Marimanti Ward, Tharaka South, Tharaka Nithi County, CAVIMA promotes practical, inclusive and sustainable community action.

CAVIMA is a community-based, non-political and non-profit organization established for lawful community development purposes.

Our community commitment

We combine local knowledge with practical training, responsible partnerships and evidence-informed action. Our aim is to strengthen community capacity so that people can participate in solutions, protect natural resources and pursue resilient livelihoods.

Training philosophy

CAVIMA treats training as a two-way learning process. Facilitators bring technical information, while community members bring local experience, questions and practical realities. Sessions should be understandable, participatory and connected to action.

Training community organizations

We can support local organizations with governance, meeting procedures, role clarity, record keeping, activity planning, budgeting basics, communication, accountability and documentation.

Farmer learning

Farmer sessions can include crop husbandry, soil health, water conservation, nursery management, climate adaptation, pest and disease awareness, post-harvest handling and market-oriented planning.

Youth mentorship

Youth programmes can combine life skills, leadership, digital awareness, agriculture, enterprise thinking, environmental stewardship and exposure to positive role models.

Women-focused learning

Women can benefit from practical sessions on livelihoods, group organization, savings culture, agriculture, enterprise development, household nutrition and leadership participation.

Training methods

We favor demonstrations, field learning, group discussion, peer learning, question-and-answer sessions, simple guides, follow-up visits and reflection meetings rather than one-off lectures alone.

Training follow-up

Training becomes valuable when participants apply it. CAVIMA therefore encourages simple action plans, follow-up conversations, peer support and documentation of lessons learned.

Working with CAVIMA

We welcome constructive engagement with farmers, community groups, schools, youth and women groups, researchers, volunteers, government extension structures, civil society organizations, private-sector actors and development partners. Any formal collaboration is subject to appropriate agreement, roles, approvals and documentation.
