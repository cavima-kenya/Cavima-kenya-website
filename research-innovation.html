Rooted in Ntujia, Marimanti Ward, Tharaka South, Tharaka Nithi County, CAVIMA promotes practical, inclusive and sustainable community action.

CAVIMA is a community-based, non-political and non-profit organization established for lawful community development purposes.

Our community commitment

We combine local knowledge with practical training, responsible partnerships and evidence-informed action. Our aim is to strengthen community capacity so that people can participate in solutions, protect natural resources and pursue resilient livelihoods.

Why community research matters

Research should help communities make better decisions. CAVIMA encourages questions that arise from real community challenges and seeks practical findings that can improve programmes and livelihoods.

Participatory inquiry

Community members can help define research questions, identify indicators, collect observations and interpret findings. This improves relevance and builds local ownership of learning.

Agricultural trials

Small demonstrations can compare practical options such as mulching methods, water-saving techniques, crop varieties or nursery practices. Results should be interpreted carefully and not presented as universal conclusions without adequate evidence.

Climate and environment

Community monitoring can document rainfall experiences, soil and water challenges, tree survival, land-use concerns and local adaptation practices. Such information can guide conservation and resilience programming.

Digital learning

Simple digital tools can support surveys, record keeping, photo documentation, mapping, training materials and communication where connectivity and skills permit.

Innovation through local solutions

Innovation does not always mean expensive technology. It can mean improving a process, organizing a group differently, adapting a proven technique or combining local knowledge with technical advice.

Evidence and accountability

CAVIMA can use baseline information, activity records, participant feedback, before-and-after observations and outcome indicators to understand progress.

Sharing knowledge

Useful findings should be returned to communities in accessible language and shared responsibly with partners, while respecting privacy, consent and the limits of the evidence.

Working with CAVIMA

We welcome constructive engagement with farmers, community groups, schools, youth and women groups, researchers, volunteers, government extension structures, civil society organizations, private-sector actors and development partners. Any formal collaboration is subject to appropriate agreement, roles, approvals and documentation.
