Who We Are

A community-rooted organization serving people, families, farmers, youth and women in Tharaka Nithi County and beyond.

Protect Nature. Empower People. Transform Community.

Rooted in Ntujia, Marimanti Ward, Tharaka South, Tharaka Nithi County, CAVIMA promotes practical, inclusive and sustainable community action.

CAVIMA is a community-based, non-political and non-profit organization established for lawful community development purposes.

Our community commitment

We combine local knowledge with practical training, responsible partnerships and evidence-informed action. Our aim is to strengthen community capacity so that people can participate in solutions, protect natural resources and pursue resilient livelihoods.

Our identity

CAVIMA stands for Community Action for Values, Innovation, Mentorship, and Advancement. It is a community-based, non-political and non-profit organization established to support lawful community development purposes in Tharaka Nithi County and, where appropriate and permitted, other areas.

Our roots

Our work is connected to the everyday realities of households and community groups. We recognize that local people understand their challenges deeply. Our role is to listen, facilitate learning, connect ideas and resources, strengthen organization and help communities move from discussion to practical action.

Vision

To build a united, empowered, economically resilient and environmentally responsible community where people have stronger opportunities to improve their livelihoods and participate in local development.

Mission

To mobilize community members and resources for sustainable social, environmental and economic development through agriculture, training, research, savings, livelihoods, youth and women empowerment, community projects and responsible partnerships.

Core values

Integrity, honesty, transparency, accountability, equality, respect, teamwork, discipline, inclusiveness, responsibility, community service, fairness, non-discrimination, confidentiality and sustainability guide our work.

Governance and accountability

CAVIMA seeks to maintain clear leadership responsibilities, appropriate records, participatory decision-making and responsible management of community resources. Organizational structures and activities should remain consistent with applicable Kenyan requirements and approved internal governance documents.

Where we work

Our home area is Ntujia village, Marimanti Ward, Tharaka South, Tharaka Nithi County. We aspire to learn from and collaborate with communities across Kenya where our skills, partnerships and resources can add value.

Working with CAVIMA

We welcome constructive engagement with farmers, community groups, schools, youth and women groups, researchers, volunteers, government extension structures, civil society organizations, private-sector actors and development partners. Any formal collaboration is subject to appropriate agreement, roles, approvals and documentation.
