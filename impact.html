Rooted in Ntujia, Marimanti Ward, Tharaka South, Tharaka Nithi County, CAVIMA promotes practical, inclusive and sustainable community action.

CAVIMA is a community-based, non-political and non-profit organization established for lawful community development purposes.

Our community commitment

We combine local knowledge with practical training, responsible partnerships and evidence-informed action. Our aim is to strengthen community capacity so that people can participate in solutions, protect natural resources and pursue resilient livelihoods.

What impact means to CAVIMA

Impact is more than counting meetings or participants. We seek evidence of meaningful changes in knowledge, practices, participation, livelihoods, environmental stewardship and community resilience.

Agricultural outcomes

Possible indicators include households reached with agricultural learning, farmers applying improved practices, functioning demonstration activities, nursery establishment, tree survival and improved farm planning.

Environmental outcomes

CAVIMA can track trees planted and surviving, conservation activities, community awareness, waste-reduction initiatives and local actions that protect soil and water.

Training outcomes

We can monitor participants trained, skills demonstrated, follow-up actions, organizational improvements and participant feedback.

Youth and women outcomes

Progress may include participation in training, leadership roles, enterprise initiatives, volunteering, mentorship engagement and increased confidence in community decision-making.

Community outcomes

CAVIMA can document community groups supported, outreach activities, referrals and partnerships, while recognizing that broader development outcomes are influenced by many factors.

Learning and reporting

Results should be documented honestly, including challenges and activities that did not work as expected. Learning helps improve future programming.

Long-term vision

The ultimate aim is stronger communities that can organize, learn, protect their environment, improve livelihoods and pursue opportunities with greater confidence and resilience.

Working with CAVIMA

We welcome constructive engagement with farmers, community groups, schools, youth and women groups, researchers, volunteers, government extension structures, civil society organizations, private-sector actors and development partners. Any formal collaboration is subject to appropriate agreement, roles, approvals and documentation.
