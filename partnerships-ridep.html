Rooted in Ntujia, Marimanti Ward, Tharaka South, Tharaka Nithi County, CAVIMA promotes practical, inclusive and sustainable community action.

CAVIMA is a community-based, non-political and non-profit organization established for lawful community development purposes.

Our community commitment

We combine local knowledge with practical training, responsible partnerships and evidence-informed action. Our aim is to strengthen community capacity so that people can participate in solutions, protect natural resources and pursue resilient livelihoods.

Partnership principle

CAVIMA values partnerships that are transparent, practical, respectful and aligned with community needs. Collaboration should have clear roles and realistic expectations.

Learning from Kenyan practice

Kenya has many organizations working in agriculture, environmental conservation, food security, livelihoods and community development. CAVIMA seeks to learn from approaches that have demonstrated value while adapting them to local realities.

RIDEP Kenya and learning

RIDEP Kenya is an organization whose publicly described work includes community development, food and nutrition security, environmental conservation, training and agriculture-related activities. CAVIMA can use such organizations as learning and networking references.

Potential collaboration areas

Possible areas for collaboration include farmer training, agroecology, environmental restoration, community mobilization, organizational capacity building, learning exchanges and joint knowledge activities, subject to agreement between organizations.

Respecting institutional independence

Mentioning a potential or learning relationship does not mean CAVIMA has a formal partnership. Any formal partnership should be confirmed through appropriate organizational communication and documentation.

Resource mobilization

CAVIMA welcomes responsible partners who can contribute technical expertise, training, equipment, learning resources, networking, mentorship or lawful financial support.

Partnership accountability

Partnership activities should define objectives, responsibilities, reporting arrangements, safeguarding expectations and learning indicators.

Building Kenyan networks

CAVIMA is interested in constructive relationships with CBOs, farmer organizations, government extension structures, research institutions, universities, private enterprises, civil society organizations and development partners.

Working with CAVIMA

We welcome constructive engagement with farmers, community groups, schools, youth and women groups, researchers, volunteers, government extension structures, civil society organizations, private-sector actors and development partners. Any formal collaboration is subject to appropriate agreement, roles, approvals and documentation.
