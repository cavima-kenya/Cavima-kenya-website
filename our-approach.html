Protect Nature. Empower People. Transform Community.

Rooted in Ntujia, Marimanti Ward, Tharaka South, Tharaka Nithi County, CAVIMA promotes practical, inclusive and sustainable community action.

CAVIMA is a community-based, non-political and non-profit organization established for lawful community development purposes.

Our community commitment

We combine local knowledge with practical training, responsible partnerships and evidence-informed action. Our aim is to strengthen community capacity so that people can participate in solutions, protect natural resources and pursue resilient livelihoods.

Community ownership

We begin with the community. Activities should respond to identified needs and should strengthen local ability to continue useful work beyond a project period.

Participation

Community members should have meaningful opportunities to contribute ideas, make decisions, implement activities and review results.

Practical learning

We connect training to demonstrations, action plans, follow-up and reflection so that knowledge can become practice.

Evidence-informed decisions

CAVIMA encourages the use of credible technical information, community observations, simple data and lessons learned when deciding what to do next.

Inclusion

Women, youth, vulnerable households, farmers and other relevant groups should be considered in programme design and participation.

Environmental responsibility

Activities should consider their effects on soil, water, biodiversity, trees and other natural resources.

Accountability

Clear roles, records, transparent communication and responsible stewardship of resources are important to maintaining community trust.

Partnership and sustainability

We seek partners who strengthen rather than replace community capacity. Long-term value is considered in project planning, training, maintenance and local ownership.

Working with CAVIMA

We welcome constructive engagement with farmers, community groups, schools, youth and women groups, researchers, volunteers, government extension structures, civil society organizations, private-sector actors and development partners. Any formal collaboration is subject to appropriate agreement, roles, approvals and documentation.
