CAVIMA Training

People, knowledge and practical action.

CAVIMA supports practical learning for farmers, community organizations, youth groups, women's groups and other stakeholders who want to strengthen agricultural, organizational and community-development capacity.

Farmer training

Possible training areas include crop production, horticulture, livestock, irrigation, soil health, climate-smart practices, pest and disease management, post-harvest handling, value addition and agribusiness.

Training community organizations

CAVIMA can help organizations strengthen group management, leadership, planning, community mobilization, record keeping, project design, monitoring, reporting, proposal development and partnership readiness.

Training organizations across Kenya

Where capacity and programme arrangements allow, CAVIMA can share knowledge with CBOs, farmer groups, youth groups, women's groups, self-help groups and other organizations seeking practical capacity building.

Practical demonstrations

Participants learn more effectively when information can be connected to practice. Sessions can use demonstrations, exercises, field visits, group discussion, case studies and peer learning.

Mentorship

Mentorship can help participants apply lessons after training. Follow-up conversations can identify challenges, reinforce skills and reveal additional learning needs.

Training requests

Organizations can contact CAVIMA with the topic, location, target group, expected participants and preferred period. Requests can be considered according to technical requirements, available capacity and programme arrangements.

Work with CAVIMA

We welcome responsible conversations with community organizations, farmers, researchers, institutions and development partners interested in practical and sustainable community development.
