Rooted in Ntujia, Marimanti Ward, Tharaka South, Tharaka Nithi County, CAVIMA promotes practical, inclusive and sustainable community action.

CAVIMA is a community-based, non-political and non-profit organization established for lawful community development purposes.

Our community commitment

We combine local knowledge with practical training, responsible partnerships and evidence-informed action. Our aim is to strengthen community capacity so that people can participate in solutions, protect natural resources and pursue resilient livelihoods.

A community organization with a practical purpose

CAVIMA — Community Action for Values, Innovation, Mentorship, and Advancement — is a community-based organization rooted in Tharaka Nithi County, Kenya. We work toward stronger households and communities by connecting agriculture, environmental conservation, social empowerment, savings culture, skills development, research, mentorship and responsible partnerships.

Our community context

Communities in semi-arid and drought-affected areas can face pressure from unreliable rainfall, soil erosion, limited household incomes, food insecurity, unemployment and limited access to technical information. CAVIMA seeks to respond through locally relevant activities that strengthen people's capacity to identify challenges, organize resources and build practical solutions.

Agriculture, livelihoods and food

Our community agenda recognizes agriculture as both a source of food and an important livelihood pathway. CAVIMA promotes crop production, nurseries, tree planting, livestock opportunities, water-conscious farming, soil protection, household nutrition and learning that can help families make better production decisions.

Environmental responsibility

CAVIMA has supported tree-planting and biodiversity activities, including Neem, mango, cypress and macadamia seedlings. These efforts respond to drought, soil erosion, environmental degradation and the need for productive landscapes that can provide shade, food, ecological benefits and future livelihood opportunities.

Learning and mentorship

We believe sustainable change requires knowledge that people can understand and use. CAVIMA therefore supports community conversations, farmer learning, youth mentorship, organizational training and practical demonstrations. Our goal is not simply to deliver information, but to help communities apply it.

A platform for collaboration

CAVIMA welcomes constructive relationships with community groups, farmers, schools, technical professionals, researchers, civil society organizations, private-sector actors and development partners. Collaboration is approached with transparency, clear roles, respect for community priorities and attention to measurable results.

Our commitment

We aim to remain community-centered, non-political, inclusive and accountable. We value integrity, honesty, transparency, responsibility, teamwork, respect, fairness, sustainability and service to the community.

Working with CAVIMA

We welcome constructive engagement with farmers, community groups, schools, youth and women groups, researchers, volunteers, government extension structures, civil society organizations, private-sector actors and development partners. Any formal collaboration is subject to appropriate agreement, roles, approvals and documentation.
