Who we are

Local action with a long-term vision.

CAVIMA is rooted in Ntujia Village and focuses on practical projects that respond to local needs while creating opportunities for young people and vulnerable members of the community.

Our purpose

We bring community members together to improve livelihoods, protect the environment, support children, strengthen savings and promote responsible, inclusive community development.

Our direction

Vision: To build a united, empowered, economically resilient and self-reliant community in Tharaka Nithi County.

Mission: To mobilize community members and resources for sustainable social and economic development through savings, livelihoods, community projects, capacity building, mutual support and partnerships.

Core values: Integrity, transparency, accountability, equality, respect, teamwork, discipline, inclusion, community service and sustainability.

From nursery to community

The nursery is central to CAVIMA's environmental work. Seedlings are raised and cared for with the goal of increasing tree cover and improving the appearance and resilience of targeted areas.

CAVIMA also promotes responsible reuse and recycling of materials such as milk packaging/nylon and paper, linking environmental care with practical community action.

Next growth areas: goat rearing and fish farming, alongside stronger livelihood, youth empowerment and partnership programmes.

Why we plant trees

CAVIMA's tree-planting work responds to drought and environmental pressure in the area. The organization aims to improve local climate resilience, reduce soil erosion as trees mature, create future food sources through fruit and nuts, and expand green cover.

The work is ongoing: seedlings continue to be raised so that more areas can be restored and communities can participate in long-term environmental stewardship.
