Who we are

CAVIMA is a community-focused organization that seeks to bring practical knowledge closer to people. Our work is built around listening to communities, understanding local priorities, building useful skills and supporting solutions that can continue beyond individual activities.

Agriculture and livelihoods

We promote agricultural learning covering crop production, horticulture, livestock, irrigation, soil and water management, post-harvest handling, value addition, agribusiness and market-oriented approaches. We see agriculture as both a source of food and an opportunity for sustainable livelihoods.

Training organizations and communities

CAVIMA can provide training and capacity-building support to farmers, CBOs, self-help groups, youth groups, women's groups and other community organizations. Training can be adapted to the needs of the participants and can combine workshops, demonstrations, discussion, peer learning and mentorship.

Research for positive change

CAVIMA promotes practical research that starts with community questions. Research findings can be translated into training, demonstrations, information and improved programme decisions so that knowledge contributes to action.

A bridge for collaboration

Sustainable development often requires many actors. CAVIMA welcomes responsible collaboration with community organizations, researchers, educational institutions, government stakeholders, private organizations, NGOs and development partners.

Work with CAVIMA

We welcome responsible conversations with community organizations, farmers, researchers, institutions and development partners interested in practical and sustainable community development.
