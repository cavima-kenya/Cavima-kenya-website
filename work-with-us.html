Rooted in Ntujia, Marimanti Ward, Tharaka South, Tharaka Nithi County, CAVIMA promotes practical, inclusive and sustainable community action.

CAVIMA is a community-based, non-political and non-profit organization established for lawful community development purposes.

Our community commitment

We combine local knowledge with practical training, responsible partnerships and evidence-informed action. Our aim is to strengthen community capacity so that people can participate in solutions, protect natural resources and pursue resilient livelihoods.

Volunteer

Volunteers can support community mobilization, event organization, agriculture demonstrations, environmental activities, communications, research assistance and youth mentorship, depending on skills and safeguarding requirements.

Technical contribution

Agronomists, community development practitioners, researchers, educators, digital professionals and other specialists can contribute knowledge through training, mentorship, technical review or learning exchanges.

Institutional partnership

Organizations can work with CAVIMA on agreed activities such as community training, research, environmental conservation, livelihoods, farmer empowerment or organizational strengthening.

Support community initiatives

Responsible supporters may contribute lawful resources, equipment, learning materials, technical services or other agreed support. CAVIMA values transparency and proper documentation.

Student and research engagement

Students and researchers may engage in community-based learning or research where appropriate approvals, consent, ethical requirements and organizational procedures are respected.

How to start

Introduce yourself or your organization, explain the area you would like to support, describe the value you can bring and provide your preferred contact details. CAVIMA can then discuss whether there is a suitable pathway for engagement.

Working with CAVIMA

We welcome constructive engagement with farmers, community groups, schools, youth and women groups, researchers, volunteers, government extension structures, civil society organizations, private-sector actors and development partners. Any formal collaboration is subject to appropriate agreement, roles, approvals and documentation.
