Agriculture & Food Security

Strengthening household food production, sustainable farming, nutrition and local agricultural value chains.

Rooted in Ntujia, Marimanti Ward, Tharaka South, Tharaka Nithi County, CAVIMA promotes practical, inclusive and sustainable community action.

CAVIMA is a community-based, non-political and non-profit organization established for lawful community development purposes.

Our community commitment

We combine local knowledge with practical training, responsible partnerships and evidence-informed action. Our aim is to strengthen community capacity so that people can participate in solutions, protect natural resources and pursue resilient livelihoods.

Food security begins at household level

CAVIMA views food security as a combination of availability, access, utilization and resilience. Community activities therefore encourage households to strengthen production, diversify foods, improve storage and make informed choices that fit local conditions.

Sustainable crop production

Training can cover land preparation, seed and planting material selection, spacing, soil fertility, integrated pest management, water management, harvesting and post-harvest handling. Farmers are encouraged to combine local knowledge with reliable technical guidance.

Nurseries and tree-based farming

CAVIMA promotes nursery development and tree planting as a way to support environmental restoration and productive landscapes. Trees can contribute to shade, fruits, soil protection, biodiversity and long-term household assets when the right species are matched to local conditions.

Livestock and future diversification

The organization has identified livestock opportunities such as goat rearing and other appropriate enterprises as areas for future development. Such activities can complement crop farming and provide additional livelihood options when managed responsibly.

Nutrition-sensitive agriculture

Food production should contribute to healthier households. Community learning can promote diverse diets, kitchen gardens, safe food handling and the link between farming decisions and household nutrition.

Water and soil management

Because drought and erosion can limit agricultural productivity, CAVIMA encourages practices such as mulching, contouring where appropriate, soil-cover management, water harvesting, efficient irrigation and protection of water sources.

Market awareness

Farmers need more than production skills. We encourage learning about demand, quality, collective action, simple records, value addition, pricing awareness and responsible links to buyers and service providers.

Working with CAVIMA

We welcome constructive engagement with farmers, community groups, schools, youth and women groups, researchers, volunteers, government extension structures, civil society organizations, private-sector actors and development partners. Any formal collaboration is subject to appropriate agreement, roles, approvals and documentation.
