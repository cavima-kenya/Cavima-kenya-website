CAVIMA • Agriculture & Food Security

A long-form programme perspective.

Strengthening food systems through practical farming knowledge, water management, livestock, markets and value addition. This page is intentionally detailed so visitors can understand the organization, its methods and the opportunities for participation.

01. Crop production

CAVIMA can strengthen practical agricultural knowledge through field demonstrations, farmer groups, records and responsible extension-style learning. Topics can include crop husbandry, soil health, pest and disease observation, water management, post-harvest handling, diversification and market orientation.

02. Horticulture

03. Nursery management

Nursery management is an important part of CAVIMA’s wider community-development model. The organization can approach this area through participation, practical learning, responsible planning, documentation and collaboration. Activities should be adapted to community priorities, available resources, technical requirements and applicable Kenyan laws and procedures.

05. Water and irrigation

Water management is central to resilient agriculture. CAVIMA can promote water harvesting, storage, efficient irrigation, moisture conservation and production planning that matches available water. Technical designs should be checked by qualified personnel and adapted to site conditions.
