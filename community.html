CAVIMA Community

People, knowledge and practical action.

CAVIMA seeks to create opportunities for communities, youth and women to build skills, participate in decisions, strengthen livelihoods and contribute to local development.

Youth in agriculture

Young people can participate in farming, agribusiness, digital agriculture, value addition, agricultural services, research, technology and community leadership.

Women and livelihoods

Women contribute strongly to food production, household livelihoods, marketing and community organizations. CAVIMA promotes access to knowledge, skills, leadership and economic participation.

Community mobilization

Development activities are stronger when people understand the purpose, contribute ideas and participate in implementation. Meetings, groups, discussions and peer learning can support this process.

Entrepreneurship

Communities can explore enterprises linked to agriculture, processing, services, input supply and marketing. Planning, records, market understanding and responsible financial decisions remain important.

Community service

Collective action and volunteering can help address practical local challenges. Activities should be responsibly planned, inclusive and appropriate to community needs.

Inclusion and dignity

CAVIMA promotes respectful participation and seeks to avoid exclusion. Community members should have meaningful opportunities to contribute according to their interests, abilities and circumstances.

Work with CAVIMA

We welcome responsible conversations with community organizations, farmers, researchers, institutions and development partners interested in practical and sustainable community development.
