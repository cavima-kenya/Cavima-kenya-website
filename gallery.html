Documenting activities

Photographs can show nursery work, farming, training, meetings, community service, environmental activities and other CAVIMA initiatives.

Write accurate captions

A good caption should explain what is happening, where appropriate, without exaggerating results. Keep dates, locations and activity descriptions accurate.

Respect and consent

Before publishing identifiable photographs of people, obtain appropriate permission and respect privacy. Use clear, relevant photographs that represent CAVIMA activities honestly.

Work with CAVIMA

We welcome responsible conversations with community organizations, farmers, researchers, institutions and development partners interested in practical and sustainable community development.
