CAVIMA About

People, knowledge and practical action.

CAVIMA seeks to contribute to knowledgeable, resilient, productive and empowered communities by connecting people with practical learning, research, innovation, agriculture and sustainable development approaches.

Our vision

To become a trusted community organization contributing to sustainable, resilient, knowledgeable and economically empowered communities through agriculture, research, innovation and inclusive development.

Our mission

To empower communities with practical knowledge, skills, research-based information and opportunities that improve agricultural productivity, strengthen livelihoods, protect the environment and promote sustainable community development.

Our objectives

CAVIMA's programme direction includes agricultural capacity building, community research, organizational strengthening, environmental stewardship, youth and women's participation, food-security awareness, livelihood development, knowledge sharing and partnership building.

Our philosophy

We believe communities should participate in identifying their challenges and developing solutions. Local knowledge is valuable, and it becomes even more useful when combined responsibly with research, technical expertise and appropriate innovation.

Integrity and accountability

CAVIMA aims to communicate accurately, use resources responsibly, respect communities and document activities honestly. Claims about achievements, partners and results should be supported by appropriate records.

Learning and improvement

Community development is a learning process. We encourage feedback, reflection, monitoring and adaptation so that future activities can become more relevant and effective.

Work with CAVIMA

We welcome responsible conversations with community organizations, farmers, researchers, institutions and development partners interested in practical and sustainable community development.
