CAVIMA Partnerships

People, knowledge and practical action.

CAVIMA welcomes responsible collaboration with community organizations, farmers, researchers, educational institutions, government stakeholders, private organizations, NGOs and development partners.

Why partnerships matter

Community challenges can require technical expertise, networks, resources and learning from several actors. Constructive partnerships can strengthen reach and reduce unnecessary duplication.

Community organizations

CAVIMA can engage CBOs, self-help groups, farmer groups, youth groups and women's groups around training, knowledge sharing, community activities and organizational strengthening.

Research and education

Universities, colleges and researchers can contribute technical knowledge, research methods, students, innovation and evidence while communities contribute local experience and priorities.

Government and county stakeholders

Where appropriate, collaboration with relevant public institutions can help align initiatives with local priorities, technical guidance and available programmes.

Private sector and development partners

Responsible collaboration can support technology, markets, enterprise development, training, environmental activities and other community priorities.

How to approach CAVIMA

Potential partners can share their organization profile, location, proposed activity, target community, expected contribution and intended outcomes. Each opportunity can be reviewed according to capacity, priorities and appropriate arrangements.

Work with CAVIMA

We welcome responsible conversations with community organizations, farmers, researchers, institutions and development partners interested in practical and sustainable community development.
