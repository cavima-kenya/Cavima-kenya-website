Rooted in Ntujia, Marimanti Ward, Tharaka South, Tharaka Nithi County, CAVIMA promotes practical, inclusive and sustainable community action.

CAVIMA is a community-based, non-political and non-profit organization established for lawful community development purposes.

Our community commitment

We combine local knowledge with practical training, responsible partnerships and evidence-informed action. Our aim is to strengthen community capacity so that people can participate in solutions, protect natural resources and pursue resilient livelihoods.

Taking information closer

Outreach bridges the distance between organizations, technical information and households. CAVIMA can use village meetings, farmer groups, demonstrations, schools and community events.

Listening first

Good outreach begins with listening. Community members can identify needs, barriers, opportunities and locally trusted ways of communicating.

Awareness and mobilization

Outreach can raise awareness on agriculture, environment, nutrition, youth participation, savings culture, community responsibilities and available learning opportunities.

Demonstration-based outreach

Practical demonstrations often make technical messages easier to understand. Small plots, nurseries, tree-planting activities and household-level examples can support learning.

Referral and linkage

Where CAVIMA cannot directly provide a service, it can help communities identify appropriate technical institutions, government services, civil society partners or other lawful support channels.

Feedback and accountability

Community members should have opportunities to provide feedback on activities. CAVIMA aims to use feedback to improve programme quality and accountability.

Working with CAVIMA

We welcome constructive engagement with farmers, community groups, schools, youth and women groups, researchers, volunteers, government extension structures, civil society organizations, private-sector actors and development partners. Any formal collaboration is subject to appropriate agreement, roles, approvals and documentation.
